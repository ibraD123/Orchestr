The problem

Most listings lose the sale before anyone reads them.

Image generators make pictures. They don't know why your product isn't selling, they can't write Arabic, and they stop at a file on your desktop.

Blind spots

Nobody audits 3,000 SKUs by hand

Missing size charts, one-photo listings and English-only titles stay live for months because no one has time to check every product on every marketplace.

Unread signals

Buyers already told you what's wrong

"Runs small", "colour isn't like the photo", "doesn't last". It's in the reviews and return reasons, written in four dialects, and nobody turns it into a fix.

Broken Arabic

AI images can't spell in Arabic

Generators draw Arabic as disconnected, reversed letters. For a Saudi or Emirati buyer, that reads as a cheap, untrustworthy brand.

Questions

What sellers ask us.

Which marketplaces does Basira work with?

Amazon (Saudi Arabia, UAE and Egypt), Noon, Salla, Zid and Shopify. You can also export any fix as files for channels we don't connect to yet.

Will Basira change my listings without asking?

No. Basira proposes fixes. A person on your team or your client approves each one before it is published, and every earlier version can be restored.

Where does the review data come from?

From the official marketplace connections where they include reviews and returns, and from the review and return reports you export from seller centres where they don't.

Can AI images misrepresent my product?

That's the main risk with generated images, so every image is checked against your original photo for shape, colour and logo. Anything that drifts is flagged and never sent for approval.

Do I need a designer?

No. Upload your logo, colours and fonts once, and every template uses them. Designers on your team can adjust any template and lock the parts that must not change.
